# Warranty Coverage

## Hiking boots and footwear
Hiking boots carry a 2 year warranty against manufacturing defects such as sole separation, broken eyelets, and seam failure. Normal wear of the tread is not covered.

## Tents and shelters
Tents carry a lifetime warranty on poles and zippers and a 3 year warranty on fabric and seams. Damage from misuse, fire, or animals is not covered.

## Electronics
Headlamps and other electronics carry a 1 year warranty. A device that stops working or will not charge within the first year is replaced at no cost.

## How to file a warranty claim
The customer provides the order number and a short description or photo of the defect. Support opens a warranty ticket and the replacement ships within 3 business days of approval.
