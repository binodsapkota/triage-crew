# Loyalty Tiers

## Tier benefits
Standard members earn 1 point per dollar. Gold members earn 2 points per dollar, get free expedited shipping, and have a 60 day return window. Platinum members earn 3 points per dollar and get a dedicated support queue with priority handling on every ticket.

## Escalations for loyalty members
Tickets for gold and platinum members are created with at least high priority. Platinum member issues must receive a first response within 4 business hours.
