# Damaged or Defective Items

## Reporting damage
Items that arrive damaged or broken must be reported within 14 days of delivery, and a photo of the damaged item and shipping box speeds things up. Support opens a high priority damage ticket so the warehouse can ship a replacement.

## Replacement or refund
For damaged deliveries, the customer chooses a free replacement shipped expedited or a full refund. The customer does not need to return the damaged item unless the warehouse requests it.

## Carrier damage claims
When the shipping box shows damage in transit, support files a carrier claim alongside the replacement. The customer is never asked to wait for the carrier claim to be resolved.
