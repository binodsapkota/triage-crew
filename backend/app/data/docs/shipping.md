# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
Standard shipping arrives in 3 to 5 business days. Expedited shipping arrives in 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern ship the same business day.

## Late or delayed deliveries
If an order is more than 3 business days past its promised delivery date, the customer may choose a full refund of shipping costs or a $15 store credit. Delays caused by carrier hub closures or severe weather are tracked with the carrier, and the customer receives a revised ETA by email.

## Lost packages
A package is considered lost when tracking has not updated for 7 business days. Lost packages are reshipped at no cost or refunded in full, at the customer's choice. Support must open a carrier claim ticket for every lost package.

## Tracking
Tracking numbers are emailed when the label is created. A status of label created means the warehouse is still packing the order and it has not been handed to the carrier yet.
